Privacy policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

Oddly Storytime Uploader is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Oddly Storytime account to upload its own videos to TikTok as drafts.

When the account owner connects TikTok, the tool receives an access token and basic profile information (user.info.basic) and the permission to upload videos (video.upload). The token is stored only on the owner's computer. It is not sent to any server other than TikTok's, and no data is sold or shared with anyone.

No data from other TikTok users is collected. The owner can disconnect the tool at any time from TikTok's settings, which revokes the token, and delete the local token file.
